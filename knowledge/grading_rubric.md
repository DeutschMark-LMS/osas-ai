# DMA Sales Page & Copy Grading Rubric

When a student submits copy or a sales page for review, evaluate it strictly and objectively against this 100-point rubric. Provide both an analytical scorecard and immediate rewritten examples.

## Scoring System (Total: 100 Points)

### 1. Headline & Subheadline (20 Points)
- **Schwartz Awareness Calibration:** Does the headline meet the prospect at their exact State of Awareness (Most Aware, Product Aware, Solution Aware, Problem Aware, or Unaware)? If targeting an unaware market, does it use Identification rather than a premature product claim?
- **Verbalization Quality:** Does it utilize one of Schwartz's 38 headline verbalization techniques (e.g. measuring size/speed, paradox, prime demonstration, sensory sharpening)?
- Does it stop the reader cold and compel them to read the very next sentence?
- *Grade deductions: Mismatched awareness level, vague claims, feature-led headlines, lack of emotional punch.*

### 2. The Lead & Emotional Resonance (15 Points)
- Does the opening paragraph hook into the reader's current reality?
- Does it clearly articulate the problem better than the prospect can themselves?
- Does it begin building **Gradualization** (a stream of micro-agreements and "Yes" responses)?
- Is the tone conversational and direct, avoiding robotic corporate fluff?

### 3. The Big Idea & Unique Mechanism (20 Points)
- **Schwartz Sophistication Check:** Is the copy calibrated to the market's Stage of Sophistication? If Stage 3 or 4, is there an explicit **NEW MECHANISM** answering the reader's skepticism ("How does it work?")?
- Does the copy explain WHY other past attempts failed (removing blame from the buyer)?
- Does it use **Mechanization** and **Concentration** to dismantle alternative solutions without coming across as spiteful?

### 4. The Grand Slam Offer Stack & Value Equation (20 Points)
- **The Hormozi Value Equation Check:** Does the copy maximize the Dream Outcome & Perceived Likelihood, while driving Time Delay & Effort/Sacrifice to near zero?
- Is it positioned as a "Grand Slam Offer" where the prospect feels irrational saying no?
- Are deliverables framed as vivid transformations rather than dry hours/modules?
- **Strategic Bonus Rule:** Do the bonuses solve the *next consecutive problems* created by the main purchase?
- Does the offer stack make the price feel like a 10x bargain?

### 5. Proof, Believability & Risk Reversal (15 Points)
- Are there tangible claims, screenshots, case studies, or verifiable specifics?
- Is the guarantee audacious and confidence-inspiring (reversing the risk entirely onto the seller)?

### 6. Friction, Urgency & Call To Action (10 Points)
- Is the CTA crystal clear with one single goal?
- Is the reason to act *today* legitimate and urgent?

---

## Output Format for Student Reviews:
1. **Overall Grade:** Letter Grade (A+, A, B, C, D, or F) and Total Score (/100).
2. **The Founder's Quick Verdict:** A 2-3 sentence executive summary of the sales page's core strengths and fatal bottlenecks.
3. **Category Breakdown:** Bullet points detailing what worked and what was lost in each rubric area.
4. **The 3 Fatal Leaks:** The top 3 conversion killers that must be fixed before launching.
5. **The DMA Rewrite Section:** Take the weakest 2-3 sections of the student's copy (especially the Headline, Lead, or CTA) and provide the exact high-converting DMA version.
