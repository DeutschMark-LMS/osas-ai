# $100M Leads by Alex Hormozi — DMA Master Playbook

## Core Philosophy: The Lead Generation Engine
A business cannot starve if it has an endless stream of qualified leads. Leads are the lifeblood of customer acquisition. In the DMA philosophy, we don't wait for luck or passive referrals—we build systematic, predictable acquisition engines.

---

## 1. What is a Lead & The Lead Magnet Formula
- **Lead:** A person who has shown interest and whose contact info you have permission to reach out to.
- **Engaged Lead:** A person who has consumed your hook/content and taken an explicit micro-commitment.

### The Lead Magnet Rule of One:
A world-class lead magnet **solves one narrow problem completely**, which immediately exposes the *next* logical problem that your paid core offer solves.

### The 4 Types of Lead Magnets (Ranked by Perceived Value):
1. **Software / Interactive Tools (Highest Perceived Value):** Calculators, diagnostic quizzes, automated spreadsheets, chrome extensions.
2. **Services / Free Micro-Audits:** Free 10-minute landing page audit, cold email sequence teardown, funnel inspection.
3. **Information & Blueprints:** Checklists, swipe files, battle-tested templates, video mini-trainings.
4. **Physical Deliverables:** Free-plus-shipping book, physical merchandise, sample kits.

---

## 2. The Core 4 (The 4 Fundamental Ways to Get Leads)

Every lead generation pipeline on earth falls into one of these four categories:

| Vector | One-on-One | One-to-Many |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Warm** | **Warm Outreach** (DMs, emails, calls to existing network/list) | **Free Content** (Social media, YouTube, podcasts, newsletters) |
| **Cold** | **Cold Outreach** (Cold email, targeted LinkedIn/IG DMs, cold calls) | **Paid Ads** (Meta, Google, YouTube, TikTok ads) |

### Channel 1: Warm Outreach
- Leverage the existing network: past clients, friends, followers, email list.
- Re-activate dead leads with low-friction check-in hooks: *"Hey [Name], are you still looking to [achieve dream outcome] this quarter?"*

### Channel 2: Cold Outreach
- High volume + razor-sharp personalization.
- **The ACA Formula for Cold Messages:**
  - **A — Acknowledge:** Specific observation about their business or recent post.
  - **C — Compliment / Context:** Genuine praise or common connection.
  - **A — Ask / Asset:** Offer a free value asset (lead magnet or audit) with zero sales pressure: *"I made a 3-minute video showing 2 leaks on your sales page. Mind if I drop the link here?"*

### Channel 3: Free Content (The Organic Engine)
- **The Golden Rule:** *Give away the secrets for free; sell the implementation, accountability, and speed.*
- Document, don't just create: Share real student case studies, receipts, breakdowns, and mistakes.
- Content must contain a clear Hook (0-3s), Retention Story (3-45s), and Frictionless CTA (comment a keyword for the asset).

### Channel 4: Paid Advertising
- Turn \$1 into \$3 by advertising high-converting lead magnets or direct-response VSLs.
- Test 5-10 hook angles per campaign (Contrarian, Curiosity, Bleeding Neck, Case Study).

## 3. The 4 Lead Getters (The Scale Multipliers)
Once you master the Core 4, you scale lead generation through these four multipliers:
1. **Customers (Referral Loops & Word of Mouth):** Turn happy buyers into active affiliates with structured referral rewards.
2. **Employees:** Hire media buyers, SDRs, and appointment setters to run the Core 4 full-time.
3. **Agencies:** Contract specialized partners to manage paid traffic or outbound pipelines.
4. **Affiliates & Joint Ventures:** Partner with non-competing businesses that already own your target audience.

## 4. The Rule of 100 (The Antidote to Lack of Leads)
When students complain about not getting leads, DMA MSI diagnoses their volume against the Rule of 100:
- **100 Primary Actions per Day for 100 Days Straight:**
  - 100 cold reachouts per day, OR
  - 100 minutes of high-value content creation per day, OR
  - \$100/day in paid advertising spend, OR
  - 100 warm contacts reached per day.
- Lack of leads is almost never a technical problem; it is an effort and consistency bottleneck.

## 5. DMA Hook & Lead Capture Integration
When writing ads, content, or emails for students:
- Never ask for the sale on step 1 of cold traffic. Ask for the opt-in or conversation.
- Use keyword triggers: *"Comment 'AUDIT' and I'll send you the exact checklist."*
- Ensure every lead magnet has an immediate **One-Time Offer (OTO)** or fast-action calendar booking on the thank-you page.

## 6. The $100M Leads Squeeze / Landing Page Architecture Blueprint

When evaluating, auditing, or writing squeeze pages for students, enforce this exact high-converting anatomy:

### 1. Above The Fold (The 5-Second Conversion Window)
- **Super-Header / Eyebrow:** Call out the exact target avatar (e.g. *“Attention Agency Owners & High-Ticket Consultants:”*).
- **Primary Headline (The Big Promise):**
  - Formula: `Free [Lead Magnet Name]: How to [Dream Outcome] in [Timeframe] Without [Top 2 Hated Frustrations]`.
  - Must pass the "Blink Test": In 3 seconds, the visitor must know *what it is, who it is for, and what pain it kills*.
- **Subheadline (The Delivery Vehicle):**
  - Specifies the exact format: *"A 14-page battle-tested checklist + 12-minute video walkthrough showing the exact outbound engine we used to book 47 sales calls."*
- **Tangible Visual Asset Mockup:**
  - Never display plain digital text. Render a 3D bundle mockup (digital tablet, PDF cover, workbook, or screenshot of the spreadsheet). Tangibility drastically multiplies perceived value.
- **3 to 4 High-Desire Fascination Bullets:**
  - *Bullet 1 (The Core Shortcut):* The 3-minute audit that reveals where you’re leaking clients.
  - *Bullet 2 (The Fatal Mistake):* Why 90% of agencies fail at outbound and the 1 tweak that fixes it.
  - *Bullet 3 (The Copy-Paste Swipe):* The exact word-for-word DM template we send to qualified leads.
- **Frictionless Form Fields:**
  - **Rule:** Every additional form field cuts conversion rates by 10-25%.
  - Ask for **Email only** (or First Name + Email).
  - Only ask for Phone Number if you have human appointment setters calling within 5 minutes.
- **High-Converting Button CTA Copy:**
  - Banned words: Never use *"Submit"*, *"Register"*, or *"Download"*.
  - Use first-person ownership verbs: *"Send Me The Free Blueprint Now"*, *"Get Instant Free Access"*, *"Unlock The 100-Point Audit"*.

### 2. Trust & Proof Strip (Below The Fold)
- **Micro-Social Proof:** User count (*"Join 4,200+ marketers who use this framework"*).
- **Receipts / Screenshots:** Raw metrics, client wins, or testimonial quotes with real photos.
- **Zero-Spam Assurance:** *"100% Free. Your email is strictly confidential. Unsubscribe with 1 click."*

## 7. The Thank-You Page (The Immediate Monetization Machine)
Never treat the thank-you page as a dead end (*"Thanks, check your email"*). Hormozi's rule is that **a prospect is never more likely to take a second action than the moment they just took the first**.

Every DMA Thank-You Page must follow this 3-step sequence:
1. **Confirmation + Expectation Setting:** *"Your free guide is on its way to your inbox right now. In the meantime, watch this 2-minute video..."*
2. **The Bridge VSL:** A short video that introduces the *next problem* that the free guide will create.
3. **The Fast-Action Offer (The OTO):**
   - **Low-Ticket Option:** A \$27–\$97 impulse template pack or workshop.
   - **High-Ticket Option:** A direct calendar application: *"Want us to help you implement this 1-on-1? Book a free 15-minute Strategy Session below."*

## 8. The Customer Referral Machine & The 3 Golden Moments
Customers who love your program are your cheapest, highest-converting lead source. However, asking awkwardly ruins the relationship. Use Hormozi's **3 Golden Moments**:

### Moment 1: Post-Purchase Euphoria (Immediately After Payment)
- The endorphin rush of solving their pain is at its highest point.
- *The Script:* "Most people who join have a business partner or marketing friend who is also trying to solve this. If you want to invite 1 friend to join this cohort with you, here is a private guest pass for 50% off."

### Moment 2: The First Quick Win (Days 3 to 14)
- The moment they book their first call, write their first winning hook, or land a client.
- *The Script:* "Congratulations on landing that win! We want to help more marketers achieve the exact same thing. Who are 2 other colleagues in your mastermind who would benefit from this roadmap?"

### Moment 3: The Unsolicited Compliment
- When a client sends a message saying *"This training is amazing!"* or *"You guys overdelivered!"*
- *The Script:* "Thank you so much, that means the world! Quick question: who is one person you know who is currently struggling with [pain point] that we should send a free copy of our blueprint to?"

## 9. Cold Outreach Follow-Up Cadence & The Breakup Message
80% of sales happen on follow-up touches 3 to 5, but amateur marketers stop after 1 message. Enforce this 5-step sequence:
- **Touch 1 (Day 0):** The ACA Value Hook (Acknowledge $\to$ Compliment $\to$ Ask with free asset).
- **Touch 2 (Day 2 - The Soft Bump):** *"Hey [Name], saw you were active on [Platform]—wanted to make sure you saw this before it got buried."*
- **Touch 3 (Day 4 - The Third-Party Proof):** *"Hey [Name], just helped another [Avatar] add [Result] using this exact protocol. Thought of your page: [Link to 60s Loom/Asset]."*
- **Touch 4 (Day 7 - The Direct Question):** *"Hey [Name], are you actively looking to fix [Problem] this quarter, or is this not on your radar right now?"*
- **Touch 5 (Day 10 - The Permission to Close the File / Breakup):**
  - Reverse the pressure entirely.
  - *The Script:* *"Hey [Name], assuming this isn't a priority for you right now, so I'll close out your file and stop following up so I don't clutter your inbox. If things change and you want to look at [Mechanism], you know where to find me. Wishing you continued success!"*
  - *Psychological Effect:* Prospects hate losing status and often reply: *"Actually, I was super busy! Send it over."*

## 10. The 5 High-Performing Paid Ad Creative Formats
When drafting ad scripts in the Script Lab or advising students, deploy these 5 proven Hormozi creative angles:
1. **Direct-To-Camera (The Plain Truth):** Founder speaking directly to phone camera. No fancy studio lighting. High intimacy, pattern-interrupt hook in first 2 seconds.
2. **Screen-Share Proof & Audit:** Recording Loom/screen demonstrating real numbers, live dashboard metrics, or live copy critiques. Believability is 10x higher than spoken claims.
3. **Whiteboard / Visual Diagram:** Explaining why the old model fails using simple markers and diagrams. Visual learners comprehend mechanisms instantly.
4. **Testimonial Mashup:** Rapid-fire 3-second cuts of real students holding checks, showing Stripe dashboards, or sharing before/after milestones.
5. **The Tweet / Notes App Screenshot:** High-contrast text image highlighting a bold contrarian quote, followed by a voiceover breakdown.

## 11. Affiliates & Joint Ventures (Other People's Audiences - OPA)
Why build an audience from zero when someone else already spent 5 years building your exact starving crowd?
- **The Ideal Partner:** Sells non-competing complementary products to your exact avatar (e.g., An accounting firm for agencies, a CRM tool, a design studio).
- **The 50/50 Front-End Deal:** Give the affiliate partner 50% to 100% of front-end revenue to incentivize heavy email blasts. You monetize on the backend continuity and masterminds.
- **The Co-Branded Lead Magnet:** Create an exclusive asset named for their audience (e.g. *"The [Partner Name] Agency Scaling Blueprint"*).
